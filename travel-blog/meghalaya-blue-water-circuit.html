Day 1: Noida to Umiam Lake & Shillong Arrival Saturday, Nov 28
05:30 AM Morning Leg

Noida Residence → Delhi Airport (T3) → Guwahati

DEL ⇄ GAU Non-Stop

Pre-booked cab from Noida to T3 (~1 hr). Direct morning flight to Guwahati Airport (GAU). Meet your dedicated driver with Maruti Ertiga / Innova at terminal exit.

Afternoon Scenic Leg

4-Lane Highway Climb & Sunset at Umiam Lake

Ascend smooth NH6 through Khasi pine hills (~2.5 hrs). Stop at picturesque Umiam Lake (Barapani) for evening golden-hour photos, tranquil water vistas, and hot chai.

Night 1 Stay (Shillong): Premium family room at Hotel Heiga / Windermere / Kaizun B&B. Dinner: City Hut Dhaba (North Indian specialties).
Day 2: City Aesthetics, Air Force Museum & Markham Valley Sunday, Nov 29
Morning Leisure

Ward's Lake Stroll & Optional Markham Valley Excursion

Tranquil morning stroll around colonial Ward's Lake over flat cobbled paths. Wooden bridge photos & duck feeding for your son. Optional scenic pine drive toward rolling Markham Valley.

Afternoon Culture & Nature

Air Force Museum & Paved Walk at Elephant Falls

Visit Upper Shillong Air Force Museum (real fighter jets, missiles, uniform exhibits — thrilling for kids). Head to Elephant Falls for a gentle 3-tiered paved walkway with guardrails.

Night 2 Stay (Shillong): Same hotel in Shillong. Relaxed packing and early rest for the Jaintia Hills crossing tomorrow.

Day 3: Sapphire Waters of Jaintia Hills & Riverside Beach Monday, Nov 30 (PTO 1)
PTO Morning Jaintia Hills

Scenic Drive to Krang Suri Waterfall & Natural Pool

Scenic 2.5-hour drive through rolling Jaintia grasslands. Descend a paved stone path (15 mins, zero joint strain) to India's bluest turquoise cascade pool. Safe, guided swimming with lifejackets for father & son!

Family Safety: Lifejackets mandatory for pool entry; crystal clear water is gentle and well-managed by local tourism wardens.
PTO Afternoon Valley Descent

Descent to Shnongpdeng (Dawki River Valley)

1-hour scenic descent to Shnongpdeng. Check into riverside cottage with private attached bathroom. Relaxing evening along the pebble riverbank listening to gentle flowing water under starry skies.

Night 3 Stay (Shnongpdeng / Dawki): Premium riverside family cottage or Betelnut Resort (clean attached washrooms, river view).
Day 4: Crystal Glass Boating & Living Root Bridge Tuesday, Dec 1 (PTO 2)
PTO 07:30 AM Early Light

Private Wooden Boat Ride on Umngot River

Early morning private wooden boat ride on transparent waters. Water is so crystal-clear that boats cast visible shadows on river pebbles 15 ft below! Prime aerial photography & morning tranquility before day crowds arrive.

Photography Golden Hour: Calmest water surface and zero ripples occur between 07:30 AM and 09:30 AM.
PTO Midday Heritage Walk

Mawlynnong Cleanest Village & Riwai Living Root Bridge

Drive to Mawlynnong (Asia’s Cleanest Village): immaculate flower-lined paths and eco bamboo dustbins. Walk the completely flat, shaded 10-minute stone path to the Riwai Single Living Root Bridge (zero stair fatigue).

PTO Afternoon Climb to Sohra

Ascent to Cherrapunji (Sohra Plateau)

Drive up through mist-laden mountain roads (~2.5 hrs). Check into your Cherrapunji family resort with panoramic valley views.

Night 4 Stay (Cherrapunji / Sohra): Labana Hotel / Rockside Resort / San Nael La Resort (clean 3★ comfort, hot water, North Indian kitchen).

Day 5: Cherrapunji Cascades, Mawsawa Falls & Valley Views Wednesday, Dec 2 (PTO 3)
PTO Morning Tiered Cascades

Wei Sawdong Upper Viewpoint & Mawsawa Falls

Short 15-minute gentle walk to Wei Sawdong's upper viewing platform: breath-taking emerald 3-tiered falls. Followed by nearby offbeat Mawsawa Falls: tranquil crystal pools and gentle banks without arduous trekking.

Comfort Guarantee: Restrict to upper platform viewpoints; strictly avoid the steep makeshift bamboo ladder descent to protect foot arches.
PTO Afternoon Grand Vistas

Seven Sisters Falls Viewpoint & Orange Roots Lunch

Roadside panoramic vista of Nohsngithiang (Seven Sisters Falls). Pure-veg family lunch at Orange Roots (renowned for hot dosas, idlis, dal fry, roti, and fresh thalis — 100% comforting North Indian meals).

Night 5 Stay (Cherrapunji): Second night at your Sohra resort. Cozy evening with cool mountain breeze and star-gazing.
Day 6: Caves, Sacred Forest & Return to Shillong Thursday, Dec 3 (PTO 4)
PTO Morning Limestone Cave

Mawsmai Cave Exploration & Kynrem Roadside Falls

Explore illuminated, family-friendly Mawsmai Cave. Well-lit pathways showcase natural stalactites, stalagmites, and ancient limestone fossils (exciting adventure for an 8yo). View massive Kynrem Falls from the valley drive.

PTO Afternoon Ancient Forest

Mawphlang Sacred Grove & Return Drive to Shillong

Drive toward Mawphlang. Take an easy, flat stroll through the mystical, moss-laden ancient Khasi Sacred Forest with a local tribal storyteller. Smooth 45-minute drive into Shillong city.

Night 6 Stay (Shillong): Return to Hotel Heiga / Windermere in Shillong. Evening souvenir shopping at Police Bazar (local Meghalaya honey & spices).

Days 7 & 8: Strategic Transit Hub, Flight & Return to Noida Fri, Dec 4 (PTO 5) & Sat, Dec 5
PTO Day 7 Dec 4

Shillong Leisure Morning → Guwahati Airport Transit Hub

Relaxed breakfast in Shillong. Descend the 4-lane highway (~2.5 hrs) to Guwahati. Check into a clean 3★ hotel located 10 minutes from Guwahati Airport. Relaxed evening without rush.

Strategic Masterstroke: Staying next to Guwahati Airport on Friday completely neutralizes the risk of morning highway jams, landslides, or fog delays for your flight!
Night 7 Stay (Guwahati Airport Belt): Clean 3★ hotel (e.g. Hotel Palacio / Kiranshree / transit property, 10 mins from terminal).
Day 8 Dec 5

Direct Flight GAU → DEL & Pre-Booked Cab Home

Return Home

Effortless 10-minute transfer to Guwahati Airport. Morning direct flight to Delhi. Cab waiting at T3 brings your family comfortably home to Noida by early afternoon.

Sunday, Dec 6 (Rest Day): Full home rest day in Noida before the work/school week resumes.
